## Decision for review

First make one enemy intelligible and one room believable using new performed sources and truthful events. Compare a **dry control**, **A: Working Bodies** (breath, weight, textiles, wood and restrained public machinery) and **B: Loose Assemblies** (jointed composite bodies, membrane friction, interrupted resonant cavities and sparse architectural contact). These are source/performance alternatives, not approved factions or stories. Neither returns to ubiquitous furnace drones, a pitched metal hit for every creature, or a synthetic falling tone standing in for an attack warning.

The present runtime lacks most of the information needed for good enemy/world sound. The audible distinction between noticing, preparing an attack, releasing it, being interrupted and dying has no complete dispatch path. Increasing bass or switching encoding cannot supply those semantics. A convincing source also needs proper in-engine onset and mix testing: role 13 identifies the current gain ramp as a transient risk. Resolve these playback/event dependencies before judging a full new soundscape.

## Propagation, threat priority and honest hearing

**Tier C/dry:** distance/pan with a tested cull range, no reverb, direct visible-room emitter. Match all candidates under this first. **Tier 1:** shared authored `area_id` graph/door portals, three room profiles and a filtered indirect component. **Tier 2:** obstruction traces, richer multi-path/hardware spatialization only after selected engine supports it and measurements justify it. Current mixer implements only partial Tier C.

AI hearing is a gameplay query taking source position, timestamp, loudness class and portal reach; it yields last-known investigation point/confidence, never magically tracks the player because a clip happens to be playing. Muting/reduced SFX must not change AI behavior. Audible occlusion is a render/mix function; it must not govern weapon collision or awareness. Doom code illustrates this separation.

Use material `acoustic_hint` only as a default; geometry and authored area profile own travel. A metal-looking wall cannot automatically become soundproof. Room profiles start as dry/outdoor, small hard enclosure, large hard hall. Initial return decay hypotheses .2–.5 s and .6–1.1 s for the two enclosures; keep direct attack intact and highpass return around 150–300 Hz. No room tail on every contact by default. Door portal open fraction comes from actual mover state; update filter/gain smoothly rather than one-frame switching at room edge. Crossfade ambience across boundary, preserve emitter identity and do not double its power in overlap.

For a source hidden around a corner, spatialize indirect sound toward the first audible portal so apparent direction leads to a real opening; blend direct route when visible. A simple single obstruction ray can over-mute a source partially behind cover. Initial occluded attenuation band -4 to -12 dB and filter cutoff 1.5–4 kHz are hypotheses; critical windup must remain readable once attack is physically possible. Do not apply a global “always hear dangerous sound full volume” rule that reveals every remote enemy through walls. If a source is too hidden/filtered to permit reaction, level/AI must withhold the unseen instant attack or provide an authored entrance warning, rather than secretly bypass acoustics. Outdoor sources have little room return; neighboring interior sources remain portal-routed.

Distance curves must be in map units selected by movement/level scale. Start audition at 2/8/16 unit anchors, not meters: near full, tactical reduced, preview/remote further reduced/cull. Match perceived distance with high-frequency/return proportion and level; don't pitch-shift threats randomly as a substitute. Front/back and height aren't resolved by stereo pan alone. Optional directional indicator/subtitle bears uncertainty (left/right/near), never an exact wall-through silhouette. Mono must preserve rhythm/material/semantic identity even though spatial cues collapse.

Protect imminent windup and player damage above ambience/remote activity/debris. Role 13 proposes 24 total effects with soft groups 4 local weapon, 4 critical, 10 impacts/voice, 4 world/travel, 2 UI; accept this as one initial shared policy, not another extra 24 here. Borrow idle capacity with semantic ranking then audibility/distance/age. Never let a quiet unnecessary ambient loop consume a reserved danger slot. Music and reverb returns are separate bus work. World audio owns diegetic/local ambience and its area returns; music owns the distinct score bus and bar-safe state transitions. Immediate critical-cue ducking is independent of those musical transition boundaries. The old furnace bed is unapproved historical material, not the accepted new ambience bank. A stolen critical tell logs a failure; death/pain for one actor cannot overwrite another's necessary warning. Group repeated footfalls/deaths of far crowds, retain nearest approach cue, and avoid a chorus of notice barks upon spawning a whole group. Rate-limit incidental activity, not required attack tell. Coordination calls describe actual ordered state and never sound if canceled. AI/encounter schedule limits dangerous simultaneous releases; mixer priority alone cannot make three incompatible attacks fair.

## Explicit pause and resume policy

**P recommendation: freeze gameplay audio transport with simulation.** This is preferable to reconstructing a half-finished warning from guessed state: preserve active voice sample cursors, selected take, emitter/attack/event IDs, loop phase, area sends, reverb state, score stem positions/musical clock and current threat-envelope phase. Simulation-owned audio markers stop advancing. Pause UI sound, if enabled, uses an independent UI transport; it does not wake AI or consume gameplay warning slots. Focus loss follows the same pause policy when gameplay pauses.

Transition to pause at the authoritative simulation boundary: consume accepted events through that boundary exactly once, freeze the event consumer watermark, capture the voices/mix state, then ramp a separate final gameplay-bus gain down over an initial 5–10 ms while **holding source time**. Holding a frozen sample or last mixed block during this short envelope needs a click-safe implementation; do not advance through the next warning/release to create a fade. Output zero after the fade. Audio already queued to the device may play for one queued-buffer interval; record this latency and uncertainty rather than claim sample-perfect physical pause. The simulation cannot release an attack after its pause boundary. Engine integration must synchronize simulation event queue and audio transport under a bounded handoff, avoiding callback races.

On resume restore the same frozen state, update listener/attached emitter transforms from the frozen simulation, ramp the separate gameplay-bus gain up over an initial 5–10 ms and resume cursor/musical-clock advancement. These ramp times are audition hypotheses; use a common output envelope, not a new 15-ms onset ramp on every active voice. Do not reset shuffle bags, source variants, score loop positions or event IDs. Resume emits no new notice/tell/release event. The consumer continues strictly after its saved watermark; event IDs and generation prevent duplicate cues.

| State at pause boundary | Exact resume treatment |
|---|---|
| Enemy actively winding up, tell still playing | Resume the same take at the same cursor and same attack age; remaining preparation time and audio phase remain aligned. Pending release stays queued at its unchanged future simulation marker. Do not replay initial recognition or begin a second warning. |
| Enemy winding up, brief tell already ended | Preserve completed tell and windup state; do not manufacture a fresh audio warning. Remaining visible preparing pose and optional persistent threat caption remain. Existing design must provide enough warning at ordinary play speed; pause is not a reason to repair it with an extra bark. |
| Projectile release committed before pause | Keep committed release/event consumed even if its audible tail is frozen. Resume tail/attached travel loop and projectile at their stored phases/position. Never launch again, replay blast as new, or roll back projectile damage. Next collision creates its ordinary new contact event. |
| Attack not yet released | Resume preparing state; release happens once only if the later simulation marker actually commits. Interruption/death before that marker cancels it normally. |
| Collision/death committed at boundary | Preserve the outcome, consumed contact/death IDs and remaining voices. Resume terminal/contact tail, never play a new pain/death or resurrect a travel loop. Event ordering decides whether boundary event committed; callback timing cannot decide gameplay. |
| Background bed, room return and score | Resume stored loop/stem phases and effect state without a new source start or musical transition. Never let the score silently jump through bars while paused simulation stands still. |

Restart differs from resume: increment generation, flush queued gameplay events and all voice/loop/return state, then construct initial ambience/score from new level state. Device change/lost audio state is a separate recovery path: reconstruct *sustained* emitter/ambience/score state at saved simulation age where feasible, mark one-shot history consumed and never restart a blast/notice/death. If a mid-windup transport cannot be recovered accurately, keep the game paused until its remaining preparing state/caption and transport are restored; do not invisibly discard a required warning or silently restart the attack.

## Sources and production pipeline

Acquire one Hold/Cross family before a whole cast: 3–4 performed notice takes; 4 preparation/release/recovery sequences per role; 4 interrupt, 4 pain and 3 terminal efforts; 6 sole contacts per surface and 3 fabric/load gestures. Record full sequences to preserve performance intention, then split stems with matching IDs and approved onsets. Minimum materials: stone/mineral, wood/composite, thin metal, cloth/soft ground; armor/body contact remains role 13 ownership. Doors require three start/stop/latch performances of the actual selected mechanism. Ambience needs one dry localized loop, one outdoor bed and sparse original spots for one room, with room tone.

Use original foley or properly licensed library; performer agreement must allow game derivatives and distribution. Recording proposal: 48 kHz/24-bit dry close mic, separate room mic when useful, peaks initially -12 to -6 dBFS, no automatic noise gate chopping breaths, mic/object distance slate and room tone. Safe material substitutes or licensed fractures avoid asking someone to perform hazardous breaks/electrical acts. Source provenance fields: author/performer, exact source filename/hash, URL/provider, date, license copy/hash, consent, recording conditions, take/selected region, processing and derivative IDs. No reference-game sample extraction, copied recognizable lines or age-based rights assumptions.

Edit for performance, remove DC if present, preserve clean pre-onset and end fade, trim noise only after listening to quiet material. Keep raw native source, 48 kHz float edits and stems `voice/body/contact/return`; export current runtime mono 44.1 kHz S16 PCM. A reverb baked into every clip cannot respond to a new room; preserve dry masters. Spatial loop single-channel; future stereo bed path explicitly separate. Select one useful mic channel when stereo averaging cancels body; mono-fold test before final source commitment.

Processing: C gain/edit only. A gentle per-source EQ to retain breath/midrange and physical body; layering physical support 10–18 dB below voice initially. B low-density membrane/contact plus mildly time-stretched/formant-preserving cavity where audition justifies it; retain original-performance version for bypass. No universal octave-down, sine bass, bitcrush or echo. Synthetic noise/filtered impulses can support fictional contact/machinery; they cannot replace all efforts, footfalls and room tone. Compress only problem sources; don't equalize every effort to death-scream intensity. Dry onset warning should be within roughly 5–20 ms of its event in edited file, pending measured device latency; this is a source target, not claimed end-to-end latency.

Variation uses shuffle bags with no immediate repeats, fixed semantic envelope and bounded loudness; initial secondary contact pitch variation ±0.3–0.7 semitone and gain ±1 dB only after audition. Required tell rhythm/onset unchanged across takes. Notice repetitions have per-actor and encounter cooldown. Loops need seam inspection, phase/random start only for ambience, and start/stop tails that don't click. Never randomize windup phase. Far-crowd composites are optional later and cannot fake a specific near threat.

Normalize by a named audition reference, not a magic LUFS target for 100 ms clips. Starting relative active-window level hypotheses, with matched close player shot = 0 dB reference: close danger tell -3 to -6 dB; notice -6 to -12; release -3 to -9; pain/recovery -9 to -16; terminal effort -6 to -12; nearby footsteps -12 to -20; world mechanism -9 to -18; ambience -20 to -30. Different spectral content needs manual loudness matching; these are relative bus/source audition bands, not final dBFS peaks or safe listening levels. Delivery measured peak/true peak and headroom follow role 13; no independent bank normalization that wipes performance variation. Initial music duck for imminent cue 2–4 dB, 5–15 ms attack and 120–250 ms release; music role owns composition/bus details. If cue needs massive ducking, redesign source/density first. Optional dialogue uses safe/rest pocket or a queued repeatable log; subtitles and independent voice control, no compulsory reading during live warning.

## Level, light and story integration

Common scene uses lighting role's unchanged calibration shell V1–V4 and neutral Hold/Cross, then a connected room/vestibule route only after mechanics/acoustics exist. Assign stable area IDs to court, door threshold and recovery vestibule; actual map/material owner assigns final values. Material IDs bind impact/footstep classes; lighting area IDs map to acoustic areas where sensible but need not be one-to-one. A small bright patch inside one room doesn't create a new reverberation chamber. Geometry owns portals and cover. Source gestures imply objects that art actually includes; no invisible huge machine hum in a small empty room.

Level beat sequence: entry/rest (one distant localized source, little score) → first awareness (real actor cue, stable light) → tell/release (protect warning, no arbitrary darkness) → overlap encounter (bounded threat cadence) → death/settling (score downshift requested, atmosphere not instantly triumphal) → recovery vestibule (5–15 s sparse listening opportunity if route pace allows) → optional environmental clue → next visible entrance. This is a proposed rhythm, not time spent locking the player. Light/music receive beat event with room/source IDs; no sonic scare should extinguish the only required flank visibility. Story sound reflects consequence (damaged latch, work station ceases, abandoned object moving), not endless exposition. Decorative tension never uses the exact imminent-attack rhythm; if it repeatedly fools players into dodging nothing, discard it.

## Auditions, silence and recording

Gate 1: blind new-source C/A/B dry efforts at equal active-window perceived level, screen fixed; four random examples per role/state, then 20 repeated actions with actual approved cadence. User describes whether threatening, physically credible, distinct and tiring; source poor alone is rejected before effects.

Gate 2: same room and replay: quiet 5 s → Hold awareness/preparation/release → Cross approach/commit → real interruption → noninterrupting hit → death/support contact → door state/denial → 5 s quiet. Compare music off/on, full/default versus restrained dynamics, headphones/speakers/mono and low comfortable volume. Threats positions and timings identical across C/A/B. No extra health, louder gun or nicer visual granted to one audio candidate. Record state identification, cue-to-action response, perceived direction near/far, fatigue and preference separately. Early 2–3-person review is formative; proposed 80% semantic recognition is a starting diagnostic, no statistical validation claim.

**Silence tests:** mute music/ambience and verify threats still communicate; mute SFX and verify poses/projectile/interrupt/death/accessibility alternatives carry meaning; play quiet scene alone 60 s and count accidental threat-like gestures; stop moving and verify no footfalls; pause mid-windup and verify the frozen cursor/attack age resume together without a new cue; kill/interrupt/reset and verify no stale release, moving loop or ghost breath. A fully silent rest interval can be intentional; a failed emitter must be distinguished by logs. Mono/subtitle test preserves state identity; captions tag “ranged enemy preparing” versus “enemy fires,” with optional rough direction, and do not duplicate every toe tap. Independent SFX, voices, ambience, music sliders and reduced piercing-detail/dynamics presets should preserve essential cues.

**Recording/sync:** current silent 15 fps recorder is insufficient. Future capture combines lossless/high-quality video with device-loopback audio and timestamps; record actual sink/device/rate, render FPS and buffer. A test flash/event marker and corresponding click verify start offset, but loopback may precede actual headphone output. Measure physical output with mic or interface loopback for true device end-to-end latency when needed. Report uncertainty; deterministic offline render proves event order, not the played hardware latency. Capture event log plus PCM mix/stems as lawful diagnostic outputs, identify actual versus offline. Blind clips get randomized labels and a retained decoding key; no “A was the good one” introduction.

## Acceptance, failures and unresolved risk

| Gate | Required evidence / falsifier |
|---|---|
| Truthful semantics | One notice per real transition, one release per attack, no release after canceled attack/death, pain doesn't falsely promise interruption, death unique; any mismatch fails. |
| Warning | Player detects intended preparing role before harm during own maximum-rate fire/score; review recurring masked first attacks even if average recognition is high. No shortened warning hidden by leading silence/latency. |
| Spatial/material | Near/far and doorway route are believable; footstep and mechanism match actual material/support. Mono retains identity, optional caption handles direction. Walls don't pass clear fake attack or erase actionable warning. |
| Repetition | Full 20-action/60-second test has no recurring mechanical sameness or irritating little pitch lottery; listener preference recorded, not inferred from file count. |
| Quiet/loop lifecycle | No clicks, repeated seams, unintended constant noise, lingering canceled loops or pause/reset ghosts. Atmosphere does not impersonate critical tell. |
| Overlap/mix | Critical cue is never dropped in approved worst overlap; logged voice decisions/limiter use accompany human recognition. No universal pumping/ducking demanded for basic clarity. |
| Identity/quality | User hears a clear substantial improvement in gameplay and chooses to retain it. If C wins or all fail, stop expansion and revisit sources/performance. A source library of 200 weak clips is a failure. |
| Provenance | All production derivatives trace to lawful sources and performer terms; no imported reference assets. |
