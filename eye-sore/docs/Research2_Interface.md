# Eyesore round-two interface, HUD and automap research

Role 17 · 3 October 2026 · GPT-6.1 Sol / medium. Research only: no UI, runtime code, assets, builds or gameplay tests produced. The systems and numerical review bands below are proposals for critique.

## Decision for review

Start with **C: Stable Essentials**, a carefully drawn neutral overlay that always shows health and the selected weapon, exposes only resources the simulation actually owns, and keeps actionable sound captions independent of the audio volume. Compare it with **A: Field Margins**, a compact typographic interface with stable corner cards and a schematic survey map, and **B: Instrument Rail**, a narrow peripheral instrument system with visual gauges and an atlas map. Both original systems retain the same information, bindings and truth rules. Neither needs a character face, Doom status-bar arrangement, diegetic holograms or story-specific terminology.

The current native standard mode has no adequate in-frame resource/progression HUD: health and phase information appear in the window title. Calibration has a different overlay, incomplete weapon state and a scaling fault. A redesign needs one shared gameplay snapshot and a deliberate input-focus contract before it needs decorative art. A perfect interface also cannot repair an unreadable level; optional guidance must be evaluated after the unassisted route.

## Reference study and what it can support

**Doom RF:** released Linux source defines persistent health/armor/ammo/weapon/key widgets and face priorities, including attacker-angle reactions and damage/pickup palette shifts. **I:** stable locations and explicit priorities make simultaneous state legible; a face and palette wash are optional expression, not required engineering. **P:** stable numbers plus restrained perimeter damage, no whole-view mandatory red flash. The released source is a later reference snapshot rather than proof of every 1993 executable behavior. [id `st_stuff.c`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/id-Software/DOOM/master/linuxdoom-1.10/st_stuff.c).

**Doom RF:** automap supports pan/zoom/follow/grid and ten marks; mapped lines differ from unrevealed geometry, and ordinary secret lines are rendered as walls rather than exposed secrets. **I:** geometry knowledge and objective knowledge are separate; a map need not spoil exploration. **P:** explored boundaries, observed openings and player marks with explicit discovery state. Exact colors/keys/ten-mark cap need not carry over. [id `am_map.c`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/id-Software/DOOM/master/linuxdoom-1.10/am_map.c).

**Wolfenstein RF:** `WL_AGENT.C` updates status health/face/ammo/weapon/key/score from player changes; acquisition, damage and selection call corresponding drawing routines. **I:** small semantic state updates are enough for a readable classic interface. **P:** shared snapshots plus accepted gameplay events, never parsing audio names or animation colors. This file is not evidence of a general built-in explored automap; this audit does not attribute Doom's map to Wolfenstein. [id `WL_AGENT.C`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/id-Software/wolf3d/master/WOLFSRC/WL_AGENT.C).

**DUSK RF:** developer announcements describe a weapon wheel, improved world map, controller bindings, episode-specific classic HUD and auto-hop/auto-climb options. **I:** retro presentation and choice of access methods can coexist. **P:** direct slots and quick cycle first, with an optional wheel for a larger future arsenal. DUSK's announced world map is not proof of an in-level explored automap. No hidden HUD timing, font size or wheel pause policy is inferred. [New Blood announcement archive](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appgroupname=DUSK&appids=519860&enddate=1655071226&feed=steam_community_announcements).

**Prodeus RF:** a developer update announces an automap item. **I:** map availability itself can be game design, rather than a renderer feature. **P:** Eyesore should provide basic explored navigation from the beginning of its starter slice; withholding orientation behind a collectible would obscure the layout review. The short public update does not establish exact discovery, secret-reveal or vertical-selection behavior; those remain unknown here. [Developer automap update](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/964800/view/4581805995816731663).

**Boltgun RF:** June 2024 official patch notes add a Navigation Guide showing a next objective and recommended route, temporary by default with an always-on option, plus additional controller layouts and screen-mode confirmation. **I:** a fast shooter can preserve exploration while offering requested route help; settings/input details are product quality. **P:** opt-in brief route aid after a geometry-first attempt, with truthful lock/state invalidation. Do not confuse Boltgun 2's newer automap announcement with the original game's features. [Auroch/Focus patch notes](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2005010/announcements/), [specific patch](https://steamcommunity.com/games/2005010/announcements/detail/4209257868260801986).

**Dead Space remake RF:** EA documents remapping, toggle alternatives, menu narration, subtitle size/background/speaker controls, persistent screen dot and motion reductions. **I:** immersive fiction is compatible with adjustable, stable information. **P:** player-controlled captions/background and plain menus; no requirement for tiny angled diegetic resource readouts. These options do not prove perfect accessibility or reveal internal UI event dispatch. [EA accessibility account](https://www.ea.com/technology/news/accessibility-and-player-choice-in-dead-space).

## Resource, selection, damage and interaction truth

Health shows the authoritative post-damage integer policy (e.g. ceil positive values, zero at death), with gauge representing its declared maximum. Do not append percent if gameplay later treats 100 as points with overhealth. Low-health cue uses shape/label plus limited color; no mandatory flashing/heartbeat. Armor/status appears only after its approved mechanics/state exists. Unlimited resource says Unlimited; finite pool shows actual current/capacity if meaningful. No invented magazine/reload gauge. Accessibility infinite ammo differs from an unlimited-by-design weapon; option wording and run-summary disclosure distinguish them without cluttering normal play.

Weapon selection has explicit owned/selectable/current/pending/not-ready states. Direct numbers/cycle skip unowned weapons; selecting an unavailable slot gives a brief reason once per input press. Pending raise/lower remains visible only if the approved weapon contract has such states. Cooldown is an optional thin readiness accent, not a numerical timer by default. Resource deductions follow accepted release/shot rules; a rejected trigger cannot reduce the counter or display success. The current 1–3 unlock flags supply only a provisional current/owned snapshot.

Damage direction uses the event's actual incoming world direction transformed by **camera yaw at damage time**, quantized into front/left/right/back and frozen for its short display lifetime. Hazard/self/environmental damage uses a nondirectional cue. No guessed attacker position when the event lacks origin; do not track enemies through walls after a hit. Two simultaneous directions keep two small arcs rather than averaging to a nonexistent front attacker. Reduced-flash removes large tint and retains arcs/health decrement. Hit and kill confirmation follow role 12's visibility/outcome rules, never a green color or audio callback.

Ordinary pickup/use keeps movement. A proximity pickup accepted for +10 says +10 with updated pool, not its nominal +25 if capped; full pickup remains or is consumed according to role 07's explicit policy. Failed use says Locked: credential required, Sealed, Out of reach or Blocked only if that is the true reason; mention the named credential only when the player has learned it. Unsuccessful inputs cannot change switch art, journal or map state. Current native starter lacks E/use, keys, switches and general exits; these messages remain dormant until implemented. No lock/key icons appear in shared C, which has flat S–T–P–M–X and optional open O, no key/switch/secret/mandatory clear gate.

Objective text is a short known destination/action, optionally requested; it is not an enemy counter or mandatory kill instruction. Exit Ready requires a real completion/exit rule, not zero living enemies alone. A player may leave the shared C test while enemies survive according to role 06's ungated exit experiment; do not display Area clear as a prerequisite. Threat, exit availability and completion are three different states.

## Captions for muted/mono and low vision

Role 14's event crosswalk supplies notice, tell_begin, commit/release, interrupt, contact/pain/death and mechanism states. Caption eligibility is a semantic decision derived from perceptibility and gameplay relevance; it must survive master mute/mono, and cannot be implemented by asking whether a WAV voice is playing. Audio owns propagation/occlusion estimates; interface consumes a separate perceptible-event decision before user gain settings. Hidden enemy activation, soundtrack state and decorative ambience cannot reveal exact threats.

Examples: Enemy notices you (only when its actionable cue would be perceptible); Ranged enemy preparing — left; Enemy fires — left; Door closing — ahead; Attack interrupted; Nearby impact — behind. Distinguish preparing versus fires; notice is not imminent damage. No invented countdown, exact enemy coordinate/count/name, wall outline or attack path. Known actor label may replace generic role only after recognition policy; captions never require color to distinguish state. Captions use words plus optional coarse arrow. Mono caption direction comes from eligible world event origin, not stereo panning. If origin/eligibility is unknown, omit direction rather than guess.

P timing: insert accepted cue on next presented frame after semantic event; report simulation tick to presentation delay separately. Preparing remains while that attack's tell is active; interrupt/death/cancel removes it and may replace it with one response. Release switches the same actor/attack row to fires with proposed 0.8–1.4s hold, user adjustable. Direction is captured when event begins, avoiding a wiggling arrow while turning; an accessibility option can use continuously updated bearing only with clear testing. Ambient lines expire first; essential attack rows preempt pickup/objective text. Two-row base layout supports only the first slice's two roles; expansion offers up to four rows or grouped summary with honest role/direction. Overlap that hides an actionable windup is a failure requiring encounter/caption design revision, not silent oldest-row dropping. Do not postpone release until a preparing text's minimum reading time.

Speech subtitles, if later approved, get a separate optional lower/upper slot and speaker/background controls; impending threat captions take priority without truncating objective truth. Provide text history in pause, not a combat scrolling log. Ambient captions verbosity separate from threats/voice. Menu narration is a future platform/service dependency, not something this prototype already provides. Avoid announcing every footstep. Reduced hearing and muted runs also rely on visible actor tells/projectile cores and state changes; captions complement those, not excuse missing animation.

## Contrast and low-vision configuration

P use near-white/dark text pairs with selectable **opaque** backing, not only a thin shadow over moving imagery. Contrast is measured against the composited panel in brightest/darkest scenes, including weapon flashes; user can disable theme transparency independently. W3C's web guidance gives 4.5:1 ordinary text and 3:1 large text, plus 3:1 meaningful graphical boundaries as useful starting checks. These are web criteria applied as engineering heuristics here, not a certification that a moving game interface is accessible. [W3C text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html), [W3C non-text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html). Keep readable mixed case and distinguish 0/O,1/I; custom pixel type must pass actual small display reading, not just have a retro appearance. Color-deficiency presets complement shape/labels rather than remap red to green alone. Reticle outline/dot/size independently configurable; reduced motion removes animated menu backgrounds and gauge wobble. Enlarged UI reflows without hiding captions or numeric health. Low-vision inspection explicitly includes reading speed and glance distance on the user's actual display; a contrast ratio is insufficient.

## Input focus, pause, options and lifecycle

P route events through one input context at a time: gameplay → paused map/options/help → binding capture → confirmation, with explicit return stack. Escape opens pause/back, with Quit selected consciously; current instant quit binding is prototype behavior to replace. Full-screen map defaults paused in this single-player slice, clearly says Paused and freezes gameplay/audio according to roles 14/15. A later quick live-map overlay is an explicitly separate option; no silent surprise that enemies continue while browsing.

Opening any modal clears held fire/use/movement and releases pointer capture. Closing requires fresh fire/use press; suppress the initiating Space/click until released. Key repeats may navigate menus but never repeat restart/confirm/bind capture. Number keys in menu are menu actions only. Focus loss pauses, clears held state and releases mouse; regain shows paused screen, never auto-fires. Mouse device change/controller switch updates prompt glyphs without erasing bindings or applying stale controller input. Reconnect controller prompts safely and keeps pause until resume.

Keyboard/mouse: remappable gameplay/menu/map actions, invert Y, sensitivity, optional smoothing/off, weapon cycle, direct slots, walk toggle/hold. Controller: move/look sticks, independent aim settings delegated to movement, accessible deadzone preview, shoulder cycle and interact/fire; optional wheel on held button once needed, with freeze policy explicitly labelled. Three weapons do not require a wheel. D-pad/map selection works without cursor precision. Settings support keyboard, mouse and gamepad equally; visible focus, no hover-only descriptions, buttons sized for readable selection rather than unverified touch support.

Pause contains Resume, Map, Options, Restart checkpoint/run, Quit; distinguish checkpoint and full-run reset only when save support exists. Current runtime only full reset; label Restart run honestly. Restart asks once because it loses run progress, and never hides behind F1 toggling. Options: display/FOV/window safe area; HUD scale/contrast/reticle/damage cue; audio buses/dynamics; captions/subtitles; remapping/hold-toggle/aim; motion/flash/content. Immediate preview for safe visual choices; display-mode confirmation timeout reverts to previous working mode and can be canceled without seeing the new screen. Persist options separately from run state; loading a checkpoint cannot revert captions or remapping.

## Shared data, events and localization cost

P immutable HUD snapshot per simulation update contains generation, tick, player health/max, approved resource pools, owned/current/pending weapon and readiness, known objective IDs/status, eligible interaction target/reason, known inventory, map knowledge version and mode. Optional systems are absent, not fabricated zero values. UI interpolates cosmetic fades only; counters/state are authoritative. Snapshot and events share tick/generation; UI must not announce acquisition before the matching inventory commit. Gameplay owns rules; UI owns wording/layout; audio owns audibility estimates; caption eligibility is shared semantic policy.

Event packets reuse roles 03/04/07/12–15: ID/generation/tick, actor/action/target IDs, accepted outcome, source/impact location when known, reason/amount and cancellation token. No second release timeline for HUD. Role 16’s final packet adds source entity generation, contact index, known incoming direction, material/outcome, state-before/after and canceled attack ID; reuse these fields rather than a parallel caption packet. Presentation can lag audio due to display latency, so compare captured semantic tick, caption-present frame and actual device sound separately; do not promise identical arrival merely because both consume one event. Fan pellets group a receipt by attack/target as role 12 does; never count pellets as distinct pickup notifications. Pause freezes gameplay-age timers; UI menu animation can continue by a separate clock. Reset/checkpoint increments generation, clears stale caption/receipt queues and reconstructs map/player state from the corresponding saved knowledge policy. Replay/open-close cannot duplicate unlock or secret receipts. Failed transaction publishes no acquired/objective-complete event; staged mover can say Moving only after commit, then Open after completion.

Localization requires string IDs, plural rules and measured shaping/bidi text, not concatenated English prefixes and ASCII-only bitmap glyphs. Binding prompts use current action binding, with glyph/text fallback; no hardcoded PRESS R after remapping. Reserve roughly 50% text growth as an initial layout stress case, test long words and RTL separately; do not claim a fixed multiplier covers every language. Font fallback/atlas memory and accessibility/menu narration integrations are engine tasks. Starter scope is compact: core labels, three weapon roles, a small interaction-reason set, a small threat-caption set, options/help and one short objective. No bespoke noun vocabulary per room before story review. Rich logbook/dialogue is deferred content cost, not needed to deliver coherent fiction.

Caption `hold_ms` and visual fade are presentation properties, not attack duration. A preparing caption remains governed by tell state and cancellation even if its preferred reading hold has not elapsed. A release event changes its text immediately; user dwell settings cannot delay damage/release or preserve a false preparing label. Simulation tick, display frame and audio device frame are logged separately without manufacturing a second gameplay timestamp.

Runtime knowledge updates require a declared observation rule (visible traversable area, observed boundary, successful use, discovered secret).
