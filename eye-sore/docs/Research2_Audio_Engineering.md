### Tick, mix and heard time

Use three clocks explicitly: simulation time; monotonically advancing callback/mix frame count; platform monotonic wall clock. Mix frames count rendered PCM, not a hardware presentation clock. Gameplay transport frames freeze on gameplay pause; mix frames continue producing zero/UI. Map simulation timestamps to transport frames from one epoch using rational tick conversion/remainder accumulation, not increments rounded independently per event. Declare fixed tick period, sample rate, bounded lead, published simulation frontier and earliest unrendered transport frame. The implementation must keep required batches available before their mapped frames; a lead alone cannot guarantee this under stalls. Place an on-time one-shot at its **within-block frame offset**. This is exact digital placement under the declared mapping, not a guarantee of input-to-ear timing or synchronization between independently clocked devices. Reset/reopen creates a new mapping segment; record wall-time anchors/drift and discontinuities rather than secretly moving past event timestamps.

Unknown future collision cannot be scheduled before simulation resolves it. A packet already late to the available mix horizon starts at earliest unrendered sample and logs lateness; do not skip its attack by seeking forward to conceal delay. Preserve batch causal order when several late records share that frame. Sustained loops may recover phase separately. An asset with retained pre-onset frames has useful onset at `scheduled_start + asset_onset_offset`; file start is not useful onset. Trim/document onset through role 13; scheduling the file earlier requires an actually known earlier action and an explicit authoring policy, and cannot anticipate an unresolved contact or leak a future warning. A bounded lead is a real latency tradeoff; measure it, and never delay damage to match sound. Log simulation stamp, main-thread enqueue stamp, consumer frame/offset, asset useful-onset frame, postmix capture frame and displayed video marker. These distinguish leading silence, frame loop delay, callback quantization, backend buffering and actual output delay. Never report requested 512 frames as total input-to-ear latency.

Preallocate ordered command/ack rings and clip resources; load/decode/stream refill off the audio thread. The audio thread owns voices/cursors/envelopes. A one-producer bounded SPSC queue is an initial design only if one main-thread dispatcher covers gameplay/UI/lifecycle commands; device/worker messages route through it or require a deliberately supported multi-producer design. Publish a complete batch atomically or a bounded batch-complete marker before admission. Separate capacity for lifecycle/cancel barriers and coalescible transform updates; apply transforms before voices at their declared frame. A full queue cannot silently lose Pause/Reset/Cancel or wait inside callback. Fail the affected diagnostic and pause via a bounded controller fallback; acknowledgement timeout leaves a clear error state. Holding locks around full asset loads, logging or recording is prohibited. A repaired SDL adapter may first use short bounded device locks rather than invent a general queue, provided the same batch/barrier semantics and measured callback cost hold.

## Pause, mute, reset and error recovery

Roles 14/15 choose freeze gameplay audio transport with simulation; ordinary mute keeps transport running. Adopt that distinction under role 19's single session coordinator. Pause stops the simulation transaction path after a complete tick T, publishes its complete batch and an ordered pause barrier naming T plus transport frame P. P cannot precede already rendered PCM; a late barrier records its delay and never rewinds/replays output. The consumer acknowledges which records through T are admitted, which starts through P were rendered, and which future markers remain pending; these are not one watermark. Freeze gameplay cursors/music clock/reverb/duck state at that acknowledged boundary, retaining accepted but unrendered starts exactly once under the declared boundary policy. UI uses independent transport/capacity. Resume remaps the paused wall interval, prevents catch-up simulation and requires fresh fire/use per role 17. No release after T until resume, no completed tell replay, no blast relaunch, and a released projectile resumes its stored travel/tail while later collision remains a new event.

**Engineering correction to role 14's fade suggestion:** holding a nonzero last sample creates a nonoscillating segment; looping/replaying output history can repeat an attack and still leave a discontinuity. Neither is a specified click-free algorithm. Choose and document a finite transition process with continuity conditions and bounded state/cost; if pause fades by rendering additional gameplay samples, its pause boundary must move by that exact amount and simulation alignment must be retained. If source time must freeze immediately, use a separately validated output-only transition and label any signal extension; do not consume future warning/release samples. A known zero crossing is not guaranteed at an arbitrary pause request. On resume restore actual DSP/source state and apply one common output transition envelope, not a new 15 ms attack ramp per voice; check that a pending release's onset is not suppressed by that envelope. Initial 5–10 ms fade band is an audition hypothesis, not an accepted algorithm. Already rendered device audio cannot be recalled; report physical pause lag separately. For dry M0 the transition choice remains a small explicit engineering dependency, with no reverb/history-replay system required.

Reset increments generation before future commands, flushes old queued events/voices/returns, uses a bounded output stop envelope, initializes new listener/source/score state and starts new epoch. Global output frame clock/stat lifetime need not reset; per-generation baselines do. Old acknowledgements, decode jobs and callbacks are rejected by generation. Clip memory is released only after audio-thread acknowledgement/device close; bank swap uses immutable resources and retirement. Device close/reopen belongs off callback; controller records backend/spec/error and offers clear degraded state. Required banks failing load must block audio-quality review, rather than continue silently and claim success.

Device loss/recovery reconstructs sustained world/music at saved phase where possible; one-shot history stays consumed. Mid-windup unrecoverable transport keeps gameplay paused until state/caption/remaining cue behavior is coherent; do not restart notice or quietly allow an unwarned release. Do not promise seamless hotplug before a named backend/device scenario is measured. Keep user mute intent across pause/retry/recovery.

## Spatial implementation staged with level ownership

Begin with measured near/mid/far map-unit curves and attached moving emitter interpolation. Geometry/door state owns acoustic areas/portals; material `acoustic_hint` is an authoring default, not inferred soundproofness. Low-risk Tier 1 uses authored areas, smoothed gain/lowpass obstruction and one selected doorway route; it doesn't need ray-traced wave propagation. Portal-path direction must point toward actual opening, then blend direct when visible. Cache/rate-limit geometry queries outside callback; send acoustic parameters only. No real-time scene traversal or heap allocation in mix.

Use dry/outdoor, small hard and large hard profiles from role 14/13 as provisional returns; preserve direct onset. World sources send once to a shared room return, not individual baked reverb plus two runtime reverbs. Boundary crossfade preserves emitter IDs, phase and constant-power behavior where justified; never duplicate a source on overlap. Required cue cannot be made magically full-volume through every wall. AI/level must supply actionable warning when attack becomes possible; direct/portal attenuation then preserves appropriate reach. Simulation hearing is a separate authored stimulus system and unaffected by mute, mix attenuation or dropped voices.

Defer HRTF, complex diffraction, physically modeled multibounce acoustics and many area returns until a doorway/turn test identifies their need. Stereo pan cannot encode front/back/height fully; optional approximate directional caption is an accessibility addition, not a claim of positional precision. Music's nondiegetic creative reverb never moves with room membership.

Two capture products serve different evidence. **Deterministic digital capture:** reusable mixer function runs offline against canonical log, fixed seed/assets/gains/backend processing, writes WAV/stems offthread and renders timestamped frames. This proves event counts/sample placement/processing under that implementation; it cannot prove device latency, callback deadline or actual screen/speaker output. Floating-point/platform/backend nondeterminism means byte hashes are only promised for pinned reproducible configuration; otherwise compare declared tolerances and event identity.

**Live output capture:** record the exact final PCM buffer returned by the engine callback, including master/ceiling/clamp/quantization, plus named Linux output-monitor loopback and video timestamps. A tap before the ceiling is a separate diagnostic stem, not final output. Callback PCM is before any SDL/backend resampling or OS/device processing; it is not itself evidence of actual sink playback. Mix tap uses bounded preallocated ring, disk encoder/writer thread; dropped capture frames invalidate the affected comparison and never block callback. Each block carries start mix-frame/rate/channel count, clock anchor and generation; a gap retains its missing interval/invalid status rather than concatenating later blocks into apparently continuous audio. Loopback measures software output route, not necessarily physical loudspeaker/display delay. Capture/video/loopback clocks require start-offset and drift measurement, rate-conversion policy and markers at start/end (periodically for longer runs); one initial click cannot prove sustained synchronization. A hardware A/V measurement requires synchronized external recording or a physically connected output/input method with declared uncertainty, including its own input delay. Record audio backend/device/rate/buffer, hardware, capture route, display/FPS/vsync, source hashes, gains, master/duck/room settings, event/video/audio clocks and dropped frames. Video timestamp pacing uses wall/display time with explicit duplicate/drop policy; paused time remains visible. Frame readback/submission time is not proven display presentation time. Never speed silent simulation-dt frames into a fake timing proof.

Measured results should separate input-to-simulation, simulation-to-enqueue, enqueue-to-mix, mix-to-loopback and rendered-frame-to-display where available. Report distributions/outliers, not one mean; use no latency number until measurement exists. Optional scheduling lead, decoder starvation, callback deadline and video blocking are distinct failure counters. Human listening records device/volume/scene and cue confusions; waveform measurement can't say a gun is meaty.
