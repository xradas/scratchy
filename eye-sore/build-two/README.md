# Eyesore — three connected chapters

New Game starts a nine-level campaign: three levels each in The Pale Ward, Ash Citadel and Occupied Line, in that order. Each chapter keeps its own two creature designs and materials from the original concept boards. The previous three standalone stages remain available through Stage Select / Playtest.

The Ward leads from Intake through Containment to Breach, where a buried passage opens into the Citadel. Ash Approach, Furnace Choir and Black Reliquary lead to the civic transit conduit. Dead Platform, Civic Lockdown and Occupation Engine finish the campaign. Levels use distinct room/portal arrangements, optional branches and usable heights. See the [campaign catalog](resources/campaign/catalog.json) and [direction](docs/campaign-v3/direction.md).

The preserved standalone stages each have19 rooms and42 enemies, three weapon-bait ambushes and arena-clear manual controls, two key-return loops, two shortcuts, four side rooms, two optional secrets, physical stairs and a lift. Collecting each heavy weapon opens concealed enemy closets and safely seals the entrance; clearing the fight restores retreat. Chamfered courts, dual stair approaches, raised galleries/altars, sunken floors and split routes provide usable combat heights. Clear an arena, use its control, find the brass/red keys and return through the shortcuts to advance. Required routes do not depend on secret supplies. The Ward entrance includes the broad rear stair and containment-gate landmark.

Controls: WASD move, mouse look, left click fire,1 pistol /2 shotgun /3 melee /4 twin shotgun /5 rivet cannon /6 siege launcher, E use, Tab visited-area map, Escape pause/settings. Settings persist. Campaign Continue carries health, armor, ammunition and acquired weapons; Retry rebuilds the current level and restores its entry loadout. No jumping, dashing or reloading. Heavy weapons start unowned, with no rivets or rockets; collect their physical caches. Signed arrival caches introduce Twin in Intake, Rivet in Containment and Siege in Breach. Pressing a locked weapon key gives a cache hint. The twin shotgun fires sixteen pellets for two shells, the rivet cannon provides sustained centered fire, and the siege launcher has a wall-blocked blast that can hurt the player. The readable HUD shows acquired heavy ammunition.

Gun presentation uses authored breech/muzzle points to converge the illustrated bore toward the centered camera ray; the muzzle effect originates at the registered tip. The replacement Rivet has a consistent centered receiver across active poses. Its unchanged generated source and exact prompt are tracked in [campaign art provenance](concepts/campaign-v3/provenance.json). Actual Main checks cover accepted firing, recoil and three letterboxed window sizes; human visual/mouse review remains pending.

Flesh hits make directional blood fans and surface spatter; armor throws sparks. Ordinary deaths leave a persistent corpse, pool and small scraps. Heavy shotgun overkill scatters12–15 weighted creature-specific anatomical/hardware fragments. Intact corpses can be shot apart once; cosmetic corpse damage cannot add kills. Gore freezes under pause and clears on retry. Six species have distinct dry demonic warning/hurt/death cues. Abelian remains menu music; the Ward uses Bestial Paragon Interface and Citadel uses Dragged Through Hellfire. The Line temporarily shares Bestial pending a separate music review.

Run from source with `tools/godot.sh`, which checks Godot **4.7.2 stable**. GDScript, Compatibility renderer and matching Linux export templates are pinned.
