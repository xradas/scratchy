# Visual asset provenance

The Pale Ward concept board and current transparent creature/weapon sprite adaptations were produced with the built-in imagegen tool for this project. They are generated art, not hand-authored pixel animation. The original approved board is retained unchanged under concepts/visual-v2/corrupted-biotech; current exact edit prompts, generated originals, hashes and runtime registration are under concepts/sprite-art-v1. Runtime title uses the approved scene unchanged, with its concept HUD cropped by a Godot AtlasTexture region.

Original environment textures/geometry, muzzle-effect pixels, scripts and the preserved procedural mesh studies were authored for this project. No original Doom/Quake game graphics, models or textures were copied. The concept reference audit is recorded in docs/DOOM_VISUAL_AUDIT.md. Audio creators/licenses are credited separately in audio/CREDITS.md.

The current ceramic, steel and grate materials sample the original approved concept board directly. assets/materials/pale-ward-approved-board.png is a byte-identical copy; assets/materials/approved-material-regions.json records the exact source rectangles and hashes. Native region-local nearest sampling preserves the selected source pixels without cropping, resampling or new image generation. Floor plates use the approved steel sample. Geometry, lighting, repetition and shader relief affect the rendered appearance; exact source pixels do not establish whole-scene visual acceptance.

Earlier reference-based environment textures and the current infestation cutout were generated with built-in imagegen from the approved Pale Ward scene. Exact prompts/output paths are in concepts/environment-art-v1; PNG bytes remain unchanged. The earlier texture variants and procedural rooms are preserved.

Blood-pool and torn-creature-remains atlases are original built-in imagegen outputs, referenced to the approved Pale Ward scene. Exact prompts, original output locations, byte hashes and transparency audit are retained in concepts/gore-art-v1. PNGs are unchanged. Runtime uses only pool cells0/1 (the unused lower cells clip their source borders), and all eight remains cells. Native mesh UVs select atlas cells; shared world collision queries conform stains/remains to solid surfaces and split across risers. Creature pieces retain the source painted shading, dimmed to match the ward. These are generated raster assets, not hand-authored sprites.

The current creature atlas, assets/enemies/concept-style-v2/unsealed-atlas-r2.png, was generated with the built-in imagegen tool using the unchanged approved board as its reference. concepts/style-art-v2/generation.json and prompt.txt preserve the initial generation; revision-generation.json and revision-prompt.txt record the imagegen repair of a clipped attack claw. Both original outputs remain preserved. The runtime atlas is a byte-for-byte copy of the revised generated PNG, with no subsequent pixel transformations, cropping or resampling. Native atlas regions select its eight representative frontal poses. This generated art does not provide complete directional animation. World, viewgun and muzzle share a 320 × 180 presentation grid; human visual acceptance against the approved concept remains pending.

The versioned pistol and shotgun atlases at assets/weapons/painted-v3 were generated with the built-in imagegen tool from the earlier weapon atlases, using the approved Pale Ward board as the style reference. Exact prompts, input and output paths, and hashes are recorded in concepts/weapon-style-v3/pistol-generation.json and shotgun-generation.json with their companion prompt files. Each runtime PNG is a byte-for-byte copy of its original generated output; no subsequent raster repaint, crop, resample or compositing was applied. Generation shifted some pose positions, so authored Godot atlas regions and muzzle markers register the unchanged source pixels. assets/combat_art.json selects these versioned pistol and shotgun sources; melee and creature atlases retain their prior sources. The weapon-style review compares the unchanged concept preview and actual running-game screenshots; human visual acceptance remains pending.

The three expanded stages sample byte-identical copies of their own original approved boards at `assets/materials/expanded-stages`; exact source rectangles/hashes are in its provenance.json. Four new generated creatures at `assets/enemies/expansion-v1` use their original theme board as the reference. Prompts/original locations/hashes are in `concepts/expansion-v1/provenance.json`; reviewed native rectangles/material masks and accepted alpha audits are in `verification/expansion-v1/enemies`. Their original PNG pixels remain unchanged. Eight directional stances and frontal combat/death sequences are representative production art, with complete directional animation still pending. The three new anatomical/hardware fragment atlases at `assets/effects/gore-v2` are unchanged built-in imagegen outputs, with prompts and transparency/native-region audits under `concepts/expansion-v1/gore`.

Three heavy-gun atlases and three dedicated twelve-cell architecture atlases were produced with builtin ImageGen from the approved theme boards. Native PNG bytes remain unchanged; polygons use independently authored wall/floor/ceiling/trim/grate/door/panel/damaged/stair/infestation/banner/pillar regions. Exact prompts, reference/original paths and SHA256 hashes are in concepts/arsenal-architecture-v2/provenance.json. Strict alpha/region registration for all twelve gun phases is in verification/arsenal-architecture-v2/registration. Twin source-border and backwards launcher candidates were rejected and are not selected by runtime metadata. This is generated raster art, with authored native regions and pivots; human style acceptance remains pending.

The replacement Rivet Cannon at `assets/weapons/campaign-v3/rivet_cannon-atlas.png` was generated with builtin ImageGen from the previous Rivet as a rejected design reference and the approved Pale Ward board as the style reference. Its PNG bytes are unchanged. `concepts/campaign-v3/provenance.json` and `rivet-prompt.txt` retain the original path, SHA256, input hashes and exact prompt. Native source rectangles and authored breech/muzzle markers register the four poses. Earlier gun sources remain preserved. Campaign geometry and route graphs are authored project source; no external game levels were copied.

Pickup identity and room furnishing pass (v6): the pickup surface atlas and three theme-specific prop surface atlases were produced with built-in imagegen, using the unchanged approved theme boards as style references. Native generated PNG bytes remain unchanged. Exact prompts, original output paths, hashes and selected pixel rectangles are recorded in concepts/pickups-rooms-v6. Actual polygon ammo cases, extruded keys and fitted room equipment are authored geometry; generated surface pixels are sampled through native Godot regions. Coordinator-authored room programmes and object/progression lists under docs/room-program-v6 precede scene creation. This is generated raster artwork with authored geometry, not hand-painted animation or a claim of full concept-scene reproduction.

Presentation v8 adds triage, surgery and utility surface atlases from built-in imagegen. These are text-prompted original generated raster textures, guided by coordinator inspection of the approved Pale Ward reference; the reference image was not supplied to these generation calls. Native 1536×1024 PNG bytes are preserved. Exact prompts, original output locations, hashes and native cell regions are recorded in concepts/production-v8/material-generation.json and resources/materials/v8/room-families.json. The scoped Intake shader uses 64 native pixels/metre and nearest sampling. The HUD uses the existing approved steel region plus runtime geometric icons and numerals. Twin v8 re-registers the existing unchanged weapon atlas and uses continuous recoil of the coherent idle image; older incompatible fire/recovery images remain preserved. No new weapon raster was generated in this pass.

Staff pass v11: original infected scientist and facility security sprite atlases generated with OpenAI image_gen using the approved Pale Ward scene as style reference. Native source PNGs retained unchanged in `assets/enemies/staff-v11/`. Prompts, hashes, measured source rectangles, pivots and observed limitations are recorded in `docs/production-v11/staff-art*`. Geometry for light housings is authored in project code with existing material references.

Gore pass v12: closed volumetric chunks are original project-authored procedural meshes. Front/back source pixels reuse the unchanged approved gore atlases and staff-v11 sprites; cut surfaces and side shading are code-authored materials. No new raster image generation or image editing. Source regions, bounds and lifecycle contracts are recorded under `docs/production-v12/`.
