# Dry pickup receipts

Nine short physical receipt cues, UI bus, nonspatial and pausable. Open index.html for the 10-second type sequence and source-excerpt/designed A/Bs. No synthetic or wet layers, no music changes. These are listening candidates.

CC0 sources: Iwan “qubodup” Gabovitch, [Metal Interactions](https://opengameart.org/content/metal-interactions), and rubberduck, [100 CC0 metal and wood SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-metal-and-wood-sfx). Selected originals and existing downloaded license HTML are copied unchanged here; hashes, source paths, creator, excerpts, filters and exported files are in manifest.json. No sourcing or downloads performed for this pass.

Only item_collected produces a receipt after accepted gameplay change. Low-level ammo_added/weapon_granted events remain inventory events. Full, duplicate, paused and dead collection attempts are silent; full owned saturated bait retains trap behavior without a receipt. Aliases brass/red canonicalize to brass_key/red_key.

48 prior cues remain registered unchanged. New cues are mono48kHz PCM/Vorbis, approximately -16 to -18dBFS peak, with no added reverb. UI presentation stays under the same gameplay pause/mute/restart ownership contract. Short LUFS are not a listening judgment.
