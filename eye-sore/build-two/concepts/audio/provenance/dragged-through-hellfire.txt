 Preview:  This one is more of a brutal metal track. 
 First half is strong riff-driven heaviness. Second half is just plain evil. 
 The idea was to make something that was very heavy while maintaining an evil mood. 
 If you use it on a project or something please let me know. 
 Instruments: Guitars, bass, drums.
