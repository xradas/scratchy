 Preview:  Very guitar-driven heavy track. 
 This was another attempt to achieve a cold and violent feeling. This one got closer. 
 I made the drums a bit more spikey-sounding than the ones in Fight Them Until We Can't . 
 Use this on something cool and awesome, and let me know when you release it. 
 The zip file contains both MP3 and WAV for convenience. 
 You're probably not reading any of this, are you?. Outrageous!. 
 Main instruments: Guitar, bass, drums.
