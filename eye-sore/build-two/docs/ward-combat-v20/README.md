# Pale Ward combat pressure pass (v20)

The first two Pale Ward levels now spring authored reinforcement waves when the player accepts their key or isolation/power control. Ward 01 triggers at local power and the brass key; Ward 02 triggers at both isolation controls. Each arrival shows a 0.65-second electrical tear and plays an original, positional dry cue before the enemy becomes visible, solid, and dangerous. Arrivals use production creature art and are included in checkpoint/Continue and Retry state. Ward 03's encounter triggers are unchanged pending a human playtest there.

Across all three Pale Ward levels, main-route health falls from 350 to 201 and armor from 250 to 151. The old 25 scattered +1 pickups per family become one +1 and larger, fewer caches. Medical/armor supplies occupy alternating rooms from ammo. Optional rooms offer either shells or health; secrets retain their distinct reward. Normal pickup caps and the secret overdrive cap are unchanged.

Unsealed, Vessel, infected scientist, and infected security pursue modestly faster. Faster walk frames and a small foot-anchored movement offset make their gait more visible; attack commitment, release, damage, and interruption timing stay unchanged.

Focused verification: `tools/check_ambush_waves_v20.gd` checks arrival tell, inert phase, collision, checkpoint reconstruction, and Retry; `tools/check_progression_v13.gd` checks the objective sequence; `tools/check_ward_supplies_v19.gd` checks pickup clearance, denominations, route totals, and room separation. The actual three-level Main route completed from the ordinary campaign loadout with live damage, no secret supplies, and no route failures (`/tmp/eyesore-v20-route-three/v13-route.json`). It exited Ward 01/02/03 with 63/68, 62/80, and 80/84 kills respectively. The bot is efficient at aiming and collecting supplies, so its survival does not establish human difficulty. Human review should focus on whether the waves create pressure without unfair spawn contact, and whether the new cue and gait read under music.
