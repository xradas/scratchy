# Pale Ward lighting, loadout, pickups, and room variation (v21)

New Campaign starts with the pistol, 36 rounds, and the ammo-free melee fallback. It no longer grants the shotgun or shells at spawn. Ward Intake triage security fires a seven-pellet shotgun and drops it with three shells. A guard in the generator carries the twin shotgun, a guard in Containment culture access carries the rivet cannon, and a guard in Breach upper gallery carries the siege launcher. Each uses a visible matching gun, a matching positional firing cue, and a bounded attack: twin pellets, a rivet hitscan, or a physically colliding rocket with occluded splash. Other infected security fire pistols. Every armed guard leaves a finite weapon/ammo drop beside its body; a duplicate grants ammo. Retry rebuilds enemies and drops. Checkpoint state records collected drops and recreates only pending rewards on Continue. Creature attacks remain biological.

Health and armor packs now have distinct sizes and front values for +1, +5, +25, and +50. The rare +100 overdrive remains in a secret. Each main-route budget is 181 health and 131 armor. Larger existing kits were moved to the Ward Intake recovery stop and the Containment culture assault without increasing those totals. Ammo and medical stops are generally separate. Ordinary 100 caps and overdrive 200 caps are unchanged.

Ward Intake and Containment each have four selected room shells with new width/depth proportions. Sterile and Waste Access are long narrow halls; Processing and Purge have substantial L-shaped cutouts. Portals, objective order, and physical collision remain authored. Mounted lamps stay fixed to their visible fixtures and fade only at distant camera ranges. The first-person painted gun samples nearby live lamp colour and muzzle light, so its shading changes as the player moves through brighter and dimmer parts of the level.

Focused checks: Godot 4.7.2 import; all three Ward geometry checks; supply totals, scale, cap, and floor clearance; enemy weapon ownership/drop/Retry/Continue; fixed-fixture light checks in Compatibility; and first-person weapon light response. The physical three-level Main route passes from the new pistol-only campaign snapshot, acquires all four later guns from enemies, uses no secret supplies, and exits Ward 01/02/03 with health 48.66/68.86/46.4 respectively. The connected nine-level physical route also passes. These routes are automated and do not establish human difficulty or visual approval. Human play should assess guard gun tells, drop visibility, medical stop placement, and whether the room silhouette changes are large enough to address the boxy feel.
