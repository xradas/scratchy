# Ward Containment playtest response (v16)

The player reported that the start of the second level drew seemingly every enemy, several pieces of equipment floated, fixture textures were hard to identify, and the nine levels shared too much music.

The crowding had a specific runtime cause: entering an ordinary room called `activate_arena("")`, which woke every ungrouped actor in the stage. Ward Containment showed 74 awake actors after one second. Empty arena IDs are now ignored; a live fixture sees only the four authored opening enemies. Dormant actors acquire the player by clear physical sight within 10 metres. Accepted gunfire alerts within a weapon-specific 2–18 metre radius, blocked by walls and closed doors. Damage still wakes its target.

Four default-closed, manually operated service bulkheads divide Overlook→Culture Access, Culture Access→Cultures, Overlook→Waste Access and Waste Access→Waste Lab. They have no new key requirements. Either isolation-control order remains valid, and the cross-feed bridge stays an alternative return. The door nodes use the existing physical collision and interaction system.

Seventeen Ward Containment filtration and pipe fittings now have a visible collidable path to the floor and explicit equipment labels. The focused geometry audit checks the floor support and room footprint. This addresses the documented fixtures without claiming that every prop in the game has had a human visual pass.

Nine provisional original cues are generated by `tools/compose_campaign_v16.py`, mapped explicitly in the campaign catalog, and auditionable at `concepts/audio-v16/index.html`. The menu keeps Abelian. Each cue has its own tempo, motif, bass answer, percussion pattern and arrangement; the cues use synthesized instruments and no third-party samples. Human musical approval, longer-form development, loop transitions and warning audibility remain open.

Validation: `tools/check_enemy_hearing_v16.gd`, `tools/check_ward02_props_v16.py`, `tools/check_campaign_music_v16.gd`, the Ward Containment live route, and the exported campaign route. Automation verifies mechanisms and state; a human playtest is still needed for pacing, readability and music appeal.
