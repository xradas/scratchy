COMBAT INTEGRATION — NEUTRAL STAGE 2

Create Node3D from scripts/combat.gd, name it Combat, add under the pausable world.
Connect combat_event(Dictionary) and player_died BEFORE setup(world, player).
setup spawns unsealed at (0,.87,10), vessel at (2,.87,2.5). No main/player files were edited.
Restart: replace world, or reset() removes old actors/projectiles, collision and pending timelines and restores state.

HUD dictionary keys: health, armor, ammo_pistol, ammo_shotgun, weapon_id, weapon_phase,
phase_progress (0..1), kills, total_enemies, dead, weapon_visual_path.
weapon_phase: idle/fire/recover/switch. Combat owns camera recoil; do not apply a second kick.
Input: held left mouse (captured), 1 pistol, 2 shotgun, 3 melee. No reload/jump/dash.
Resources: pistol 18 damage/.32s, shotgun 7 x 10 damage/.85s (one shell), melee 24 damage/.5s/2.2u, no ammo.

Events all carry type:StringName, weapon_id, shot_id, position:Vector3, target_id, material.
shot adds seed, direction, pellets, physics_tick and time_seconds. Downstream HUD/audio read this authoritative event.
impact exists only for actual physics contacts and aggregates all pellets per collider/shot.
material: collider hit_material metadata; fallback hard. Enemy definitions supply flesh or armor.
Nonlethal enemy damage emits enemy_hurt; lethal emits enemy_death alone, avoiding overlapping vocal reactions.
Hurt/death add enemy_kind. Attack warning/projectile_release add enemy_kind and attack_id.
Additional events: empty (rate limited), weapon_switch, player_hurt/player_death,
encounter_started/encounter_complete, enemy_melee_release, projectile_impact.
projectile_impact is distinct from weapon impact: do not invent a pistol/shotgun sound for it.

Visual hook:
combat.configure_weapon_visuals({weapon_id:{idle:path,fire:path,recover:path,switch:path}})
HUD owns native-window weapon overlay drawing.
enemy.configure_sprite_sheet(path, columns, directions, clips, pixel_size=.015, foot_pivot=Vector2(-1,-1))
Each direction is one atlas row. Row0 is front (-Z); clockwise row numbering.
clips maps chase/windup/recovery/pain/dead to Vector2i(first_column,count).
Fixed-Y billboard preserves upright actors; chase loops, attack/pain/recovery normalize and clamp against actual committed timing; death clamps.
foot_pivot is image-space pixels within one frame. Default is bottom-center.
Sprite is offset so the foot pixel lands on capsule floor (-.85 localY); optional noncentral x pivot adjusts sprite offset.
Current capsule meshes are explicitly temporary encounter proxies; final setting/art selection remains pending.
