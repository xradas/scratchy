# Run and review the Ward redesign

Use the pinned Godot4.7.2 wrapper from the project root:

```sh
tools/godot.sh --path .
```

New Game starts the connected campaign in the rebuilt Intake. The three rebuilt Ward maps lead into the six existing later-chapter maps. The nine replacement design packets do not mean those six later playable maps have already been reconstructed.

Controls: mouse look/fire, WASD movement, E use, Ctrl crouch, Shift sprint, Tab automap, Esc pause. Weapon slots1–6 remain pistol, shotgun, melee, Twin, Rivet and Siege; a numbered slot lights when owned.

After a major objective, stop briefly at the marked checkpoint station once the landing is safe. The prompt explains whether to stop or clear nearby threats. Death offers Continue from Checkpoint; Restart Level restores the level-entry loadout. Checkpoints are RAM/session-only and are discarded by title return, New Game, level transition or closing the game. No quicksave or disk campaign save is provided.

Current Ward objectives:

- Intake: restore local utility power, find the surgical credential, return through sterile supply, survive decontamination and take the transfer lift.
- Containment: isolate culture and waste feeds in either order. One isolation opens the overhead connection; both expose the lower basin route. Killing every enemy is not an exit requirement.
- Breach: destroy the three containment anchors during the descent, then cross the rupture into the buried passage. Every damaging weapon can break an anchor; a heavy weapon is not compulsory.

Target20–25 minutes per first successful human playthrough, including normal exploration and excluding deaths. Automated routes verify physical completion and supplies, not human pacing. This target and final concept-art acceptance still need human review.
