# Arsenal, elevation and ambush pass

User request: more weapons in the Doom/Boltgun direction, more usable elevation, dedicated materials, less rectangular/serial room construction, and rooms that lure apparent progress into an arena ambush. Preserve the three theme-exclusive creature rosters; chapter placement remains deferred.

Coordinator owns reference selection, art prompts, audio design, shared contracts and final integration. Three programmers own arsenal/ordnance, stage geometry/trap state, and asset-registration/release inspection respectively. No research is delegated.

References: Bethesda's original weapon design discussion (https://bethesda.net/en-AU/news/the-art-of-doom-weapons), the Super Shotgun retrospective (https://bethesda.net/en-EU/news/top-5-weapons-of-doom-2-the-super-shotgun), and Focus's Boltgun presentation (https://www.focus-entmt.com/en/games/warhammer-40000-boltgun) support the broad direction of strongly differentiated destructive weapon silhouettes. Our roles and values below are original design decisions, rather than mechanics inferred as exact copies. No game graphics or sound are copied.

Existing pistol, shotgun and melee stay on keys 1–3. Key 4: Twin shotgun, sixteen 18-damage pellets, two shells, 1.2-second recovery; close-range crowd clearing. Key 5: Rivet cannon, centered 36-damage shots every .16 seconds, finite rivet ammo; sustained ballistic pressure. Key 6: Siege launcher, swept 26-unit/second projectile, direct 120 damage plus 90 falloff blast within 4.8 units; walls block blast and close detonations threaten the player. New guns start unowned and are acquired through physical pickups. Every accepted shot owns ammo, damage, recoil, visual flash and sound; rocket collision owns one explosion. No reload, jump or dash is introduced.

Each arena rewards an apparent progression pickup. Behind it, physical enemy closets open, the safe entry bulkhead seals, and the local roster wakes. Clearance restores retreat and enables the manual progression control. No teleported monsters, crushed players or unrecoverable gate locks. Progression must remain possible with ordinary resources and without secret supplies.

Ward: sunken culture court, raised rim and service balcony with containment hardware. Citadel: tall vaulted/chamfered stone courts, raised altar with two stairs and ring circulation. Line: sunken tracks, high platforms and overhead crossing with two stair approaches and daylight above. Three usable combat elevations and alternate routes around cover in each stage. Shared mesh/collider geometry governs floors, slopes, stairs, doors, shots and projectiles.

Twelve newly generated material cells per theme: wall, floor, ceiling, trim, grate, door, panel, damaged, stairs, infestation, banner, pillar. Dedicated flat materials replace whole-board repeated samples; original boards still guide palette, wear and forms. Native source pixels stay unchanged. New gun atlases contain idle, fire, recover and switch poses. Full original-scene visual acceptance and directional creature animation remain separate pending production work.

Dry mechanical cracks, metallic contacts and demonic pain/death remain the audio direction. New cues derive from existing licensed original library files with exact source/edit/export records. Music and existing cues remain preserved.
