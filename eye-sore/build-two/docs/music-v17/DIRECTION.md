# Eyesore score direction — v17

The soundtrack follows the place and the level objective, not a fixed metal template. Each campaign level gets one original looping cue and the menu gets an original theme. The three chapters keep a family resemblance without reusing a recording. The previous licensed Noriega songs and the v16 drafts remain in the source archive for provenance and comparison; no runtime path should select them.

| Location | Musical role | Foreground character |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Menu | Threat behind a clinical signal | Sparse, ominous motif; invites entry without a combat beat |
| Intake | Exploration and uneasy discovery | Cold air, distant machinery, restrained pulse |
| Containment | Pressure building behind sealed doors | Asymmetric industrial rhythm, bass tension, intermittent alarm texture |
| Breach | The Ward loses control | Abrasive breakbeat and heavy riff, clear battle momentum |
| Ash Approach | Siege and exposed ramparts | Slow ritual march, low drums and bell-like iron |
| Furnace Choir | Heat and mechanical worship | Furnace drone, metallic ostinato and wordless synthetic choir |
| Black Reliquary | Exploration in a hostile vault | Dark ambient procession, long spaces and rare impacts |
| Dead Platform | Deserted civic transport | Sub-bass, echoing rail percussion, low-energy dub-like pulse |
| Civic Lockdown | Occupation systems activate | Dry mechanized electro rhythm and repeating warning figure |
| Occupation Engine | Final industrial assault | Driving hybrid of hard percussion and distorted tonal force |

Composition needs contrast in attack density, instrumentation, register, rhythmic feel and harmonic movement. At least Intake, Black Reliquary and Dead Platform should leave substantial room for world sounds and enemy warnings. Reserve the densest arrangements for Breach and Occupation Engine. There is no requirement for every level to play guitar or a drum kit. Keep the mix stereo, matched at a comfortable playback level, and check the loop boundary for clicks. The review page exposes every cue individually so the user can judge appeal and ask for changes before calling it final.

Acceptance for this pass: menu and all nine campaign paths select the v17 originals, old licensed songs cannot play in normal menu/campaign/standalone flow, all ten OGGs decode and loop in a Linux export, and the chosen original score source and hashes accompany the release. Listening approval, adaptive room transitions and sustained human playtest remain open.
