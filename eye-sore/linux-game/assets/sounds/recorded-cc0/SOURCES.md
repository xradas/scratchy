# Recorded weapon source clips

The runtime weapon prototypes use short excerpts of these CC0 recordings. The original HQ preview downloads are kept beside the mono 44.1 kHz PCM game clips so the provenance stays inspectable. Excerpts only trim duration and convert format; they are not synthetic replacements.

- Pistol 1: LeMudCrab, “Pistol Shot” (real Colt 1911 recording), sound 163456, CC0: https://freesound.org/people/LeMudCrab/sounds/163456/
- Pistol 2: acidsnowflake, “Small pistol gunshot” (9 mm PAK), sound 402789, CC0: https://freesound.org/people/acidsnowflake/sounds/402789/
- Shotgun 1: LeMudCrab, “Shotgun Shot”, sound 163455, CC0: https://freesound.org/people/LeMudCrab/sounds/163455/
- Shotgun 2: Marregheriti, “Shotgun.wav”, sound 266105, CC0: https://freesound.org/people/Marregheriti/sounds/266105/
- Shotgun 3: Kodack, “Unrelenting Shotgun”, sound 258194, CC0: https://freesound.org/people/Kodack/sounds/258194/
- Arc 1–3: jensfelger, “Jacob's Ladder 50kV”, sound 367456 (recorded high-voltage arc), CC0: https://freesound.org/people/jensfelger/sounds/367456/

Freesound audio pages and author descriptions are the attribution/provenance records. Downloaded files here are the public HQ MP3 previews, not the original upload files. Weapon playback has been changed only in Furnace Descent; Starling retains its own audio set.

- Creature voices: StarNinjas, “16 Monster Growls” (mouth-performed monster growls/grunts), CC0: https://opengameart.org/content/16-monster-growls. Original ZIP and OGG takes are retained under `creatures/`; converted mono WAVs are assigned to the two Furnace Descent enemy families' tell, attack, hit, and death slots.
- General combat/UI cues: Spring Spring, “Various Sound Effects” (CC0), direct source tracks `dull_explosion.wav`, `player_hit.wav`, and `get_important_item.wav`, converted for the SDL runtime: https://opengameart.org/content/various-sound-effects-0
